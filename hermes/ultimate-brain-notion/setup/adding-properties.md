# Adding New Database Properties

Adding new properties to the [databases](https://thomasjfrank.com/docs/ultimate-brain/databases/) in Ultimate Brain is easy, and it will allow you to extend and customize your template for your own needs.

In the video below, I’ll show you exactly how to do this. We’ll work through an example, where we’ll add a new **Start Date** property to the [Projects](https://thomasjfrank.com/docs/ultimate-brain/databases/projects/) database.

In addition to simply creating the property, we’ll add a Timeline view to the Projects page in order to take advantage of the new property.

Here’s how to add a new property at a glance:

First, [unlock your target database](https://thomasjfrank.com/docs/ultimate-brain/unlocking-databases/). Then, open any page in the database, find the **Add a Property** button, and add your new property.

Once you’re done, I recommend **re-locking** your database.

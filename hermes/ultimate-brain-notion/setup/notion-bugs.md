# Troubleshooting Notion Bugs - Markdown Content

## Overview
This guide from Thomas Frank's Ultimate Brain documentation provides systematic approaches for resolving Notion issues.

## Recommended Fix Sequence

The article suggests trying these solutions in order:

1. **Test on another device** (phone, tablet, etc.)
2. **Restart the application** - Close and reopen Notion app or browser
3. **Clear page cache** - Access via `?` button (bottom-left) → More → Clear Page Cache
4. **Log out and back in** - Fully reset your Notion account session
5. **Reset Notion** - "This will not delete any of your account data, but will reset your local cache"

## Reporting Bugs to Notion

When contacting Notion's support team, include:
- Detailed description of the issue and context
- Browser type and operating system
- Current Notion version (found in the `?` menu)
- Screenshots from Developer Console
- HAR files (if applicable)

## Developer Console Guidance

The article explains how to access the Developer Console to capture error messages:

**Browser:** ⌘+⌥+J (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+J (Windows)  
**Desktop App:** ⌘+⌥+I (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+I (Windows)

Screenshots of console errors help support teams diagnose problems more effectively.
